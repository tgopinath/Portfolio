Mobile app

Kids Keyboard Adventure on a phone

A keyboard game for ages 3 to 10. KeyBot the robot explains every key, asks for keys to find, and cheers in five typing games.

  • Tap the keys on screen, or plug in a keyboard: both work everywhere.
  • Smart Difficulty moves up at 90%, stays at 70–89% and steps down with hints below 70%.
  • Stars and coins buy KeyBot hats, colours and new game worlds.
  • A parent dashboard sits behind a maths question. Nothing leaves the device.
