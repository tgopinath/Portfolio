Mobile app

Water Manager on a phone

An irrigation planner for small farms: which field to water today, how many litres and how long to run the pump, worked out from live weather.

  • Today lists the fields that need water, with litres and pump time.
  • The 7-day strip shows rain and evaporation from Open-Meteo.
  • Rain plans borewell recharge and rainwater storage before a storm.
  • Use adds up litres pumped, pump hours and electricity by field.
  • Sign in with Google to keep your farm on every device.
