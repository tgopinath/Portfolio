Brain Gym for iPhone

A native SwiftUI app (iOS 17+) that wraps the Brain Gym web app. Inside it the page switches to Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines look: large titles, a floating Liquid Glass tab bar with Home, Games, Progress, Rewards and Profile, back buttons with inline titles, and haptics. The tab bar hides while a game runs. The Android app uses Material 3 instead, and the website keeps its own playful look.

Installing an iPhone app on other people’s phones needs TestFlight or the App Store, so it is shown here as Simulator screenshots. Source: github.com/tgopinath/BrainGym-iOS ↗
