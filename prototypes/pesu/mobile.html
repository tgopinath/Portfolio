Mobile app

Pesu on a phone

Learn the phrases you need on day one in Spanish, French, German, Japanese or Hindi: hear them, say them, test yourself, and let Super Memory bring back what you miss.

  • Pick a language, then a scene such as Restaurant or Getting around.
  • Listen at normal or slow speed, tap a word to hear it alone, and press Say it to check your pronunciation.
  • The test mixes pictures, sentence tiles, typing and pairs; misses come back until you get them.
  • The phone's own voices read the phrases, so audio depends on the device.
