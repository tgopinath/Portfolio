Mobile app

Word Wonders on a phone

An animated English vocabulary builder for children aged 3 to 10: 314 words in 25 categories, learned by tapping objects that move and speak.

  • Tap the apple: it takes a bite, says its name and shows the meaning, an example sentence and a picture story.
  • Slow mode, replay, listen-and-repeat and a quick check after every word.
  • Find the Object turns each 6-word lesson into a game, with friendly hints and stars.
  • Parents get a gated dashboard: child profiles, a daily time limit and stats.
