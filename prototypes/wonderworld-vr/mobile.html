Mobile app

WonderWorld VR on a phone

Maths for children aged 5 to 12, learnt by grabbing, counting and moving real 3D objects. The same app runs on a phone, a desktop browser and, through WebXR, in a Meta Quest headset.

  • Make a child profile: a nickname, an age group and an avatar. Nothing else is asked.
  • Tap Start on Mathematics World, or enter the 3D classroom and walk through its portal. Four activities: Counting, Find the Number, Adding Up and Taking Away.
  • Drag an apple into the basket, or tap it and then tap the basket. The engine checks the objects you actually placed, gives a gentle hint after a mistake and adjusts the level.
  • Grown-ups answer a question and set a PIN to see progress. Everything stays on the device.
