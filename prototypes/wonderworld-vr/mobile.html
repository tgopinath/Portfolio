Mobile app

Anatomy VR on a phone

A 3D human-anatomy atlas for students: 2,827 named structures in 7 body systems from open anatomy data. The same app runs in a phone or desktop browser and, through WebXR, life-size in a Meta Quest headset.

  • Turn body systems on with the chips above the model, then drag to rotate and pinch to zoom.
  • Tap any part to see its name, side, size, a study note and its neighbours, then focus, isolate or hide it.
  • Cut the body with an axial, sagittal or coronal cross-section, or play the heartbeat and breathing.
  • Switch to Study for locate and name-the-part quizzes with spaced repetition. Progress stays on the device.

The parts browser for building the body organ by organ is a desktop and tablet panel.
