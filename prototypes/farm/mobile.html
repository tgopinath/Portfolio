Farm Manager on a phone

The iPhone and Android app, running with a sample farm of 11 goats, 2 cows and 155 chickens.

  • On Today, tap Mark given or Done on a reminder. It slides away, and Undo brings it back.
  • The round + adds an expense, an animal or a vaccination.
  • On Stock, one tap logs today's feed and the days left go down.
  • Tap a goat, cow or chicken tile to see that herd.

Changes stay in this browser and show up in the web app too.
