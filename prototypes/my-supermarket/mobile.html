Mobile app

My Supermarket on a phone

A 3D supermarket where children aged 5 to 12 read prices in rupees, fill a trolley, scan items at the counter, pay with play money and count their change.

  • Pick an avatar and an age band, then choose a game: free shopping, a list challenge, a budget challenge or money learning.
  • In the store, tap a product to see its price, unit and health tag, then add it to the trolley.
  • At the counter, scan each item, pay in cash, card or UPI, and count the change from the drawer.
  • ShopBuddy reads every instruction aloud, with subtitles, and gives hints when asked.
