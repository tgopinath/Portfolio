MacTalk · Incoming call

A call you can't miss, that doesn't take over

An incoming call slides in at the bottom-right corner of the screen, 360 by 104 points, with the caller, a ring and two buttons. Decline stops the ring and tells the caller; Accept opens the call window. The microphone is only switched on after you accept.

Where it appears

Screens are rendered from the app's own SwiftUI views with sample people and data. Built with Swift 6, SwiftUI and AppKit, WebRTC audio and a Supabase backend (Auth, Postgres with Row Level Security, Realtime). Source on GitHub ↗.
