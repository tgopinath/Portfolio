Mobile app

Daybook on a phone

One workspace for tasks, goals, reminders, calendar, habits, notes and personal information, with an AI assistant that understands all of it and asks before it changes anything.

  • Sign up and start with fictional sample data, or start empty.
  • Overview pulls today's tasks, habits, reminders and calendar into one page.
  • Quick capture turns plain sentences into tasks and reminders.
  • The assistant proposes changes as cards you confirm or edit.
