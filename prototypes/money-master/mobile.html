Mobile app

Money Master on a phone

A game that teaches children Indian money with real RBI specimen notes and coins at their true relative sizes.

  • Tap or drag any coin or note onto My Money; the total and a count of every pile update as you go.
  • Make Bigger Money shows the same amount in the fewest pieces.
  • Press Exchange Money to watch two ₹10 notes join into a ₹20.
  • Open Challenges for eight goals, like making ₹35 with exactly three pieces.
