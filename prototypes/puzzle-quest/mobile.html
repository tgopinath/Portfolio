Puzzle Quest on a phone

An adaptive puzzle adventure for children aged 4 to 12: eight kinds of puzzle that get easier or harder on their own, and a PIN-protected dashboard for grown-ups.

  • A grown-up sets up first: a name, an email and a 4-digit PIN, then a child profile and an avatar.
  • Pick a world on the adventure map or tap Continue playing. Each world is one kind of puzzle: jigsaws, shapes, numbers, picture stories, memory, logic, patterns and mazes.
  • Every puzzle has a hint, restart and read-aloud instructions. Two struggles make the next one gentler; three strong results make it harder.
  • Tap the lock and enter the PIN for the parent dashboard. Progress stays on the device.
