Word Garden on a phone

A card game that grows a child's English one level at a time, from matching pictures to words up to finishing sentences.

  • Tap Play on the garden map: the next level opens only when this one is finished.
  • Pick the word, find the missing letter or spell with letter tiles. A right answer flips the card to the picture and word; tap Hear it to hear it again.
  • A wrong answer shakes the card and crosses out the choice. Fewer slips, more stars.
  • Words are read by the phone's own voice. Progress stays on the device.
