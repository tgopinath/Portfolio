Mobile app

Persona3D Studio on a phone

Turn photographs of a person into a rigged, animated 3D character you can inspect, customise and export, with privacy built into every step.

  • The home screen builds a sample character in your browser; drag to rotate it.
  • The guided wizard checks each photo's lighting, blur and angle before generation.
  • Animation Studio plays 26 clips and chains them into sequences.
  • Export GLB, OBJ, PNG or a turntable video.
