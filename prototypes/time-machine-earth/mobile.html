Mobile app

Time Machine Earth on a phone

An interactive historical atlas: one map and one timeline from 3000 BCE to today, drawn from 48 documented boundary snapshots.

  • Drag the timeline or press play to watch borders change, with narration at each snapshot.
  • Tap any territory for its dates, area and what came before and after.
  • Compare two years side by side, or swipe between them.
  • Learn has quizzes built from the boundary data, and a daily challenge.
