Mobile app

Mettu on a phone

A ringtone studio in English and Tamil: cut the part of a song you love, make it loud enough, hear it ring, and set it. Nothing leaves the phone.

  • Tap Try the sample to open the editor with a tune made in the app.
  • Drag the handles: they snap to quiet points and beats.
  • Shape fades and levels it; Ring test plays it on a fake incoming call.
  • Find a song names a song from a YouTube or Spotify link.
