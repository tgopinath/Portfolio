Mobile app

India Map Explorer on a phone

A geography game for children: India's 28 states and 8 Union Territories, every district, puzzles, mini games and quizzes, on official Government of India boundaries.

  • Explore: tap any state for its capital, neighbours and districts.
  • Puzzle: drag Tamil Nadu's 38 districts into place, with hints, a timer and stars.
  • Tap த at the top to hear names and facts read aloud in Tamil.
  • Parent Corner shows what your child has learned and what to practise.
