Mobile app

Kalvi on a phone

Short daily lessons and games in Maths, English, Science and World for ages 3 to 10, with the whole app in English or Tamil.

  • Tap தமிழ் at the top to switch the whole app to Tamil.
  • Every lesson uses blocks, counters or a science demo you can move.
  • After every answer, a short card explains why.
  • Grown-ups see each child's progress behind a simple sum.
