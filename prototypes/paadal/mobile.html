Mobile app

Paadal on a phone

A music streaming app built once in Expo for iOS, Android and the web, with a full player, queue, playlists and a library saved on the device.

  • Open any mix and press play: the mini player follows you across tabs.
  • Open the player for seek, shuffle, repeat and the queue.
  • Make a playlist and add songs from any song's options.
  • Shown here with the iPhone look (Apple HIG); the Android app uses Material 3.
