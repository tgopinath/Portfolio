Kaadu காடு

A field guide to every kind of plant, grouped the way people see them: trees, shrubs, vines, herbs, grasses and bamboo, ferns, succulents, water plants, mosses, palms and conifers. A hand-written library of 70 well-known plants, many from Tamil Nadu like neem, banyan, betel vine and lotus, leads into an endless list of real plants recorded in India or worldwide on iNaturalist. Each plant page has photos with credits, facts as icon tiles, a Wikipedia summary and its sources, and you swipe to the next one. The whole app switches to Tamil, with Tamil plant names from Wikidata.
