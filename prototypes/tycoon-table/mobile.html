Mobile app

Tycoon Table on a phone

A 3D property-trading board game for 2 to 6 players. Play against AI opponents or pass one phone around the table.

  • Tap Tap to start, then pick Single Player, Local Multiplayer or the guided Tutorial.
  • Roll, buy, build, trade and mortgage from the action bar at the bottom.
  • The two top-left buttons open your portfolio and the activity feed; the receipt opens every transaction.
  • Drag to orbit the board and pinch to zoom.
