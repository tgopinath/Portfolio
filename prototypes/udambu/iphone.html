Udambu for iPhone

A native SwiftUI app (iOS 17+) that wraps the Udambu web app. Inside it the page switches to Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines look: large titles, a translucent tab bar, a back button with a centred title, sheets and haptics. The Android app uses Material 3 instead, and the website keeps its own Kolam look. It carries both levels: Explore (plain words, 3D, voice, AR Quick Look) and the Lab (the 2,827-structure 3D atlas, which works offline). Explore’s live 3D models still load from the internet.
