Mobile app

Udambu on a phone

The human body for children as young as five: tap a part on a friendly body map, turn it around in real 3D, watch it work, and hear every line read aloud.

  • Switch layers to see skin, organs, bones, muscles, nerves and blood.
  • Open a part for a real 3D model and an 8-second demo of how it works.
  • Sickness explains 35 common illnesses gently, with when to tell a grown-up.
  • Play has two games: find the part, and guess who does this job.
