SipBuddy on a phone

A water reminder that only goes away once the front camera sees you take a sip. Detection runs on the device with MediaPipe; video never leaves it.

  • Today shows the day's water as a ring and a filling drop, the next reminder and every logged sip.
  • Tap Drink now or wait for a reminder: five counted sips close it.
  • Statistics has a week and month chart, streak, 30-day average and CSV export.
  • In the Android app it uses Material 3, in the iPhone app Apple's HIG.

If the camera doesn’t start inside this frame, open the live app to try sip detection.
