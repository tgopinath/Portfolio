Mettu மெட்டு

A ringtone studio in English and Tamil, without the ads and tricks of the usual ringtone makers. Bring in a song, a share from another app or a mic recording, and trim it on a waveform whose handles snap to quiet points and beats. Fade it, level it to a standard loudness, and hear it on a fake incoming call before the Android app sets it as your ringtone, alarm or one contact's tone. Paste a YouTube or Spotify link and it names the song and points to licensed places to get it. Everything stays on the device.

Amazon Pulse

Product intelligence for sellers on Amazon India. It keeps a daily snapshot of every product it tracks, from Amazon's Selling Partner API or a seller's own CSV history, and ranks products by estimated demand for any day, week, month or year, with what entered and left the top. Trend and opportunity scores show every signal behind them, a calculator works out real profit after Amazon fees and GST with input tax credit handled correctly, and a keyword search of the Amazon.in catalog finds high-demand products to track. An AI assistant answers only from the data. Demand is an estimate from Best Sellers Rank, never shown as sales, and a period with no snapshots says so instead of guessing.

  • RoleProduct design and full-stack build
  • StackNext.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Recharts, PostgreSQL, Amazon Selling Partner API, Claude API, Vercel
  • StatusLive web app, installable on phones

Kalvi கல்வி

One learning app for children aged 3 to 10, covering Maths, English, Science and World, with the whole app in English or Tamil. A per-year age table sets round length, choices, hints and which topics open. Each topic starts with a short lesson using rendered blocks, counters or a science demo the child can move, then practice that adapts to the child. After every answer a short card explains why, and anything missed comes back later for review. Eighteen games teach as well: quizzes, coding, typing, science puzzles, a lab, short films, maps of Tamil Nadu and India, stories, music and drawing. Each child has a profile with a photo, Mani the clay elephant guides them, and grown-ups see the class level per subject.

  • RoleProduct design, content design and front-end build
  • StackHTML, CSS, JavaScript, SVG 3D renderer, Web Speech, Web Audio, d3-geo, Firebase, Capacitor

About
